Project DAWN

Project DAWN is an Ohio Department of Health program that provides free naloxone. EMSA distributes free naloxone on campus through Project DAWN, including a Narcan distribution night each semester.

Naloxone (brand name Narcan) is a medication that can reverse an opioid overdose.

Project DAWN at the Ohio Department of Health

What's in a kit

EMSA's harm reduction kits include Narcan (naloxone nasal spray), naloxone information, fentanyl test strips, and educational materials.

Kits are free and may not be resold.

Fentanyl test strips and other supplies

Along with naloxone, EMSA provides fentanyl test strips, which check a drug sample for fentanyl, plus condoms and educational materials.

With Miami's Office of Student Wellness and the Butler County General Health District, EMSA has placed 200 Narcan kits, 2,000 fentanyl test strips, 500 condoms, and educational materials in 25 fraternity houses.

Ohio law

  • Naloxone is available over the counter in Ohio.
  • Ohio's Good Samaritan Law gives limited immunity for minor possession offenses to the person who overdosed and the person who calls 911.

Ohio's Good Samaritan Law at the Ohio Department of Health

How to get naloxone

  • Pick it up at EMSA's Narcan distribution night, held each semester.
  • Or request it with the form below and arrange a pickup.
