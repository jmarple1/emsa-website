# EMSA Website: Photo Directions

For the developer. From Max Ilecki, EMS Alliance. Updated September 21, 2026.
The photos are in the attached folder, named by the letters below. Three are placeholders (marked PLACEHOLDER in the filename); originals are coming and will replace them at higher resolution.

---

## Rules for every image

- Do not publish a photo with an identifiable face until Max confirms that person's consent (status below).
- Never show: the AHA Heart-and-Torch logo, AHA course cards, Stop the Bleed or American College of Surgeons logos, children, school names, or faces of people receiving naloxone. If a crop is close, cut more, not less.
- Alt text on every image (provided below).
- Sizes: hero up to 1,600 px wide, tiles about 1,200 px, headshots square. Export JPEG or WebP, roughly 300 KB or less each. Keep the originals in the EMSA Google Drive under the entity account, not on a personal laptop.
- Hero and tiles are landscape; the trophy is portrait and stays portrait.
