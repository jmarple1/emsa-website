## 1. TL;DR

- Build a small, mobile-first site on an EMSA-owned domain (no Miami trademarks in the domain), with every account under the EMSA entity email. One job above all: a Miami student should understand EMSA and click "Join" within 60 seconds.
- Recommended platform: **Squarespace** (custom domain, officer-editable, low handoff risk). Two alternatives in §9.
- Do not custom-build the interactive pieces. **Google Forms** for the join form, class registration, and naloxone/supply requests; an embedded **Google Calendar** for events. No Eventbrite.
- Classes are **free AHA BLS and Heartsaver courses plus Stop the Bleed**. EMSA runs its own registration (Google Form); AHA eCards are purchased from and issued through the **Therapeutic Professionals** Training Center. The AHA disclaimer must appear verbatim on class pages; no AHA torch logo or card images anywhere on the site.
- Lead with EMSA's credibility assets: **2024–25 New Student Organization of the Year**, **NCEMSF member organization and New Group Initiative grant recipient**, **76+ CPR certifications**, and **200 Narcan kits and 2,000 fentanyl test strips across 25 fraternity houses**.
- No partner logos: partner names in text only.

## 5. Page-by-page outline

### Home (150–250 words · CTA: Join EMSA)
- Above the fold: EMSA logo, one-sentence description, high-contrast "Join EMSA" button, a real photo of members in action (no stock photos)
- Impact strip: "76+ students certified in CPR" · "30 trained in Stop the Bleed" · "200 Narcan kits and 2,000 fentanyl test strips across 25 fraternity houses"
- Recognition line: "2024–25 New Student Organization of the Year · NCEMSF member and New Group Initiative grant recipient"
- Three program tiles: Training · Harm Reduction · Community Outreach
- Secondary buttons: "Sign up for a free CPR class" and "Request naloxone"
- Partner names in text (no logos)

### What We Do (500–700 words · CTA varies per block)
Same structure for every block: program name, one line on what and why, who it's for, action link.

**Training**
1. **CPR & First Aid Classes.** Free AHA BLS and Heartsaver CPR/First Aid courses taught by EMSA's certified instructors, on campus, usually Sundays. Participants earn an AHA eCard valid two years. → Register
2. **Stop the Bleed.** Hands-on bleeding-control training; about 30 students trained so far. → Register / Request a session for your group

**Harm reduction**
3. **Project DAWN Naloxone Distribution.** Free naloxone on campus through Ohio's Project DAWN program, including a Narcan distribution night each semester. → Request naloxone
4. **Fraternity House Harm Reduction.** With Miami's Office of Student Wellness and the Butler County General Health District, EMSA has placed 200 Narcan kits, 2,000 fentanyl test strips, 500 condoms, and educational materials in 25 fraternity houses. → Request supplies for your house

**Community outreach**
5. **K–6 Outreach.** Personal safety fundamentals taught in classrooms at schools in the community. → Get involved
6. **AHA Heart Club.** EMSA's American Heart Association Heart Club program. → Get involved

Do not list the student EMT first response unit here. It is a long-term goal, not a program; it belongs on the About page and in the FAQ (see below).

### Join (300–400 words · CTA: interest form)
- **What you get:** hands-on emergency skills, AHA instruction, volunteer and outreach experience, a community of pre-health students, resume value for medical, nursing, PA, and EMS applications
- **Who can join:** any Miami student · no requirements · no dues · pre-health students and EMTs especially welcome
- **Time commitment:** about 1 hour a week
- **A typical semester:** 4 general body meetings, 3 CPR classes, 1 Narcan distribution night, 1 K–6 Outreach event. Join the Outreach Team if you want more.
- Interest form, then one line on what happens next (see §6)
- **Already a member on Miami Central but not active?** A short section with the GroupMe link and the next meeting date. This is the largest untapped pool (61 registered, about 15 active).
- Link to the EMSA page on Miami Central so new members also register there
- **For EMTs:** one short paragraph. EMTs help teach and staff classes and events. EMSA is not a first response unit, but aspires to build one at Miami, and EMTs who want to help shape that effort are especially welcome.
- **Open officer roles**, listed when elections approach, one line each. Doubles as recruiting.

### CPR Classes (300 words + schedule · CTA: Register)
- Courses: BLS (for pre-health and healthcare students), Heartsaver CPR/First Aid (for everyone), Stop the Bleed (for everyone)
- Free · on campus · usually Sundays · dates in the embedded calendar
- What you get: an AHA eCard, purchased from and issued through the Therapeutic Professionals Training Center, valid two years through the end of the issue month
- Registration: Google Form, one per class or one form with a date dropdown; capacity tracked in the linked Sheet
- "Request a class for your group" form for chapters, RAs, teams, and offices: group name, contact, preferred dates, course type, headcount → entity-account Sheet (MIT EMS and Cornell EMS both run this through a simple form)
- Required: AHA disclaimer verbatim (§11) · no AHA torch logo · no card images · Stop the Bleed name and logo rules (§11)

### Naloxone & Harm Reduction (250 words · CTA: Request naloxone)
- Plain explanation of Project DAWN, what's in a Narcan kit, and that kits are free and may not be resold
- Fentanyl test strips and other supplies EMSA provides, and the fraternity-house program numbers
- Ohio context, stated factually with links to the Ohio Department of Health: naloxone is available over the counter; Ohio's Good Samaritan Law gives limited immunity for minor possession offenses to the person who overdosed and the person who calls 911
- Pickup options: Narcan distribution night or arranged pickup
- Low-identification request form with a "requesting for myself / for my chapter house" field (privacy rules in §11)

### In an Emergency (150 words · CTA: Learn CPR with EMSA)
- First line: "Call 911." EMSA does not respond to emergencies.
- What to tell the dispatcher and what to do until help arrives; hands-only CPR steps; how to use an AED
- Link to Miami's AED locations **[VERIFY: does Miami publish an AED map?]**
- Naloxone basics with a link to the Naloxone page
- Closes with a "Learn CPR with EMSA" button → Register
- Model: the "In a Medical Emergency" and "AEDs on Campus" pages on Tufts EMS's site, minus anything about their own response

## 9. Platform recommendation

Max wants an EMSA-owned domain rather than a Miami URL, so the free Miami WordPress (Miami-hosted URL only) is out.

| Option | Approx. cost (verify current) | Pros | Cons | Handoff risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Squarespace (recommended)** | ~$16–25/mo + domain | Custom domain, hosting, SSL, and updates included · officers can edit without the developer · clean mobile output · native forms | Subscription · limited custom code | Low if owned by the entity email |
| B. Managed WordPress (WordPress.com or a managed host) | ~$5–25/mo + domain | Flexible, plugins, developer-familiar | Plugin and security upkeep · officers less likely to edit | Medium |
| C. Developer-built static site (Netlify/Vercel free tier) | ~$0 + domain | Fast, free hosting, full control | Only the developer can update it unless a CMS is added · content goes stale after handoff | High |

**Accounts EMSA must own, all under the EMSA entity email** (Leslie as trustee, Max as secondary): domain registrar · Squarespace or hosting · Google Calendar, Forms, and Sheets · analytics. Give the developer admin or contributor access, never ownership.

**Hosting note from the site review.** Of the 15 NCEMSF Website of the Year winners reviewed, the standalone sites that won 10 to 20 years ago mostly died or were absorbed into university pages (Villanova, Oneonta, Pitt, St. Michael's). The survivors sit on university-hosted platforms or long-lived WordPress installs with a named maintainer. A middle path to discuss with the developer: keep an EMSA-owned domain and point it at the free Miami WordPress site, so the brand is EMSA's and the hosting outlives every officer.

**Domain:** the developer chooses the name (no Miami marks in it) and registers it under the EMSA entity email, or transfers it there after purchase. The registrar account must be EMSA's, not the developer's.

## 11. Technical requirements

- **Mobile-first.** Most visitors arrive from Instagram links and QR codes at tabling. Design and test on phones first; keep the Join button reachable at all times.
- **Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA.** Alt text on all images, sufficient color contrast, keyboard navigation, descriptive link text (no "click here"), labeled form fields, captions on any video. Miami's accessible-technology policy applies WCAG AA standards to university content, and the 2024 DOJ ADA Title II rule points public entities to WCAG 2.1 AA.
- **Analytics.** A privacy-respecting tool. Track Join submissions and class registrations as the key metrics.
- **Form privacy.** Collect the minimum. On the naloxone/supply form: no name required, pickup at distribution night or a contact method of the requester's choice, a one-line note that data is not shared. Restrict Sheet access to officers. Store only under the entity account.
- **Photo rules.** Consent from anyone identifiable · no photos of children and no school names in K–6 Outreach content · no faces of people receiving naloxone or supplies · nothing showing AHA cards or the AHA torch logo. Shot list in §10.
- **Footer recognition strip on every page.** "2024–25 New Student Organization of the Year · NCEMSF member · New Group Initiative grant recipient", text only, above the disclaimers (RIT Ambulance does this with award badges).
- **Disclaimers and branding (mandatory):**
  - **AHA, verbatim on all class pages and class promotions:** "The American Heart Association strongly promotes knowledge and proficiency in all AHA courses and has developed instructional materials for this purpose. Use of these materials in an educational course does not represent course sponsorship by the AHA. Any fees charged for such a course, except for a portion of fees needed for AHA course materials, do not represent income to the AHA."
  - **AHA branding:** no Heart and Torch logo, no images of course completion or Provider cards, no wording implying AHA sponsorship.
  - **Stop the Bleed:** the name is a registered trademark licensed through the American College of Surgeons. Use it for courses taught by a registered Stop the Bleed instructor and do not use its logo without permission.
  - **Miami independence line (footer):** "EMS Alliance is a registered student organization at Miami University. The views and information on this site are those of EMS Alliance and do not represent Miami University."
  - **Medical line (footer, or on the CPR and naloxone pages):** "Content on this site is educational and is not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911."
  - **First response unit:** present it only as a long-term goal. State that it is not operating or approved and that EMSA does not respond to emergencies.
  - **Miami trademarks:** none in the domain name; do not embed or alter Miami's logo; any use of Miami marks needs approval from Miami's Trademarks and Licensing office.
