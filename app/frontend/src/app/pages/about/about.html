About EMSA

EMS Alliance (EMSA) is a registered student organization at Miami University in Oxford, Ohio. Max Ilecki founded EMSA in fall 2024. Its members are largely pre-health students, including certified EMTs.

Our mission

EMSA promotes EMS education and public health on campus through CPR, First Aid, and Stop the Bleed classes; harm reduction, including Narcan kits and fentanyl test strips; and hands-on training that builds leadership, service, and a safer campus.

Where we're headed

EMSA's long-term goal is a student EMT first response unit at Miami. That unit is not operating and has not been approved, and EMSA does not respond to emergencies. In an emergency, call 911.

Affiliations and recognition

  • American Heart Association: EMSA's instructors are certified AHA instructors, and EMSA runs an AHA Heart Club.
  • Project DAWN, Ohio Department of Health: EMSA distributes free naloxone on campus through Project DAWN.
  • Butler County General Health District and Miami University's Office of Student Wellness: partners in EMSA's fraternity-house harm reduction program.
  • National Collegiate EMS Foundation (NCEMSF): EMSA is a member organization. Its New Group Initiative grant funded EMSA's trip to the NCEMSF national conference.
  • Miami University: 2024–25 New Student Organization of the Year.
