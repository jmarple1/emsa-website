Join EMSA

Any Miami student can join. There are no requirements and no dues, and it takes about 1 hour a week.

Fill out the interest form

What you get

  • Hands-on emergency skills
  • AHA instruction from EMSA's certified instructors
  • Volunteer and outreach experience
  • A community of pre-health students
  • Resume value for medical, nursing, PA, and EMS applications

A typical semester

  • 4 general body meetings
  • 3 CPR classes
  • 1 Narcan distribution night
  • 1 K–6 Outreach event

Want more? Join the Outreach Team.

EMTs: you'll help teach and staff classes and events. EMSA is not a first response unit, but it aspires to build one at Miami, and EMTs who want to help shape that effort are especially welcome.
