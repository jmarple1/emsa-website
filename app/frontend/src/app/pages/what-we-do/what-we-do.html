Training

Free, hands-on classes on campus, taught by EMSA's certified AHA instructors.

CPR & First Aid

AHA BLS and Heartsaver CPR/First Aid, usually on Sundays. You earn an AHA eCard valid for two years.

BLS is for pre-health and healthcare students. Heartsaver is for everyone.

Harm reduction

With Ohio's Project DAWN program, the Butler County General Health District, and Miami's Office of Student Wellness.

Free naloxone

Naloxone can reverse an opioid overdose. EMSA hands it out free through Project DAWN, including a Narcan distribution night each semester.

For anyone on campus, for yourself or your chapter house.

Community outreach

Safety education for younger students, and an American Heart Association Heart Club.

K–6 Outreach

Personal safety fundamentals, taught in kindergarten through sixth-grade classrooms at schools in the community. Every semester includes an outreach event.
