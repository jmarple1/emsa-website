What we do

EMSA's programs fall into three areas: training, harm reduction, and community outreach. Each one has a way for you to take part.

  • Training
  • Harm reduction
  • Community outreach

Training

Free, hands-on classes on campus. EMSA's co-presidents, Max Ilecki and Jordan Vandeventer, are certified AHA instructors.

CPR & First Aid Classes

Free AHA BLS and Heartsaver CPR/First Aid courses taught by EMSA's certified instructors, on campus, usually on Sundays. You learn to help in the minutes before emergency responders arrive, and you earn an AHA eCard valid for two years.

Who it's for: BLS is for pre-health and healthcare students. Heartsaver CPR/First Aid is for everyone.

Harm reduction

EMSA gets naloxone and other harm reduction supplies to students, working with Ohio's Project DAWN program, the Butler County General Health District, and Miami's Office of Student Wellness.

Project DAWN Naloxone Distribution

Free naloxone on campus through Ohio's Project DAWN program, including a Narcan distribution night each semester. Naloxone can reverse an opioid overdose, so having it on hand can save a life.

Who it's for: Anyone on campus who wants naloxone on hand, for themselves or for their chapter house.

Request naloxone

Fraternity House Harm Reduction

With Miami's Office of Student Wellness and the Butler County General Health District, EMSA has placed {{ impact.fmt('narcan_kits') }} Narcan kits, {{ impact.fmt('test_strips') }} fentanyl test strips, {{ impact.fmt('condoms') }} condoms, and educational materials in {{ impact.fmt('frat_houses') }} fraternity houses, so supplies are on hand where they may be needed.

Who it's for: Fraternity chapter houses.
