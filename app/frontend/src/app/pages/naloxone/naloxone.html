Free through Project DAWN

Naloxone, often sold as Narcan, is a medication that can reverse an opioid overdose. EMSA hands it out free on campus through Project DAWN, an Ohio Department of Health program, including a Narcan distribution night each semester.

What's in a kit

Each Narcan kit contains Narcan, a naloxone nasal spray.

How to get it

  • Pick it up at the Narcan distribution night, held each semester.
  • Or request it here and arrange a pickup.

Your privacy

  • No name required.
  • We do not share the information you submit.

Ohio law

  • Naloxone is available over the counter in Ohio.
  • Ohio's Good Samaritan Law gives limited immunity for minor possession offenses to the person who overdosed and the person who calls 911.
