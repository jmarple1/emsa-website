Project DAWN

Project DAWN is an Ohio Department of Health program that provides free naloxone. EMSA distributes free naloxone on campus through Project DAWN, including a Narcan distribution night each semester.

Naloxone, often sold under the brand name Narcan, is a medication that can reverse an opioid overdose.

Fentanyl test strips and other supplies

Along with naloxone, EMSA provides fentanyl test strips, condoms, and educational materials. Fentanyl test strips check a drug sample for fentanyl. A negative result does not mean a drug is safe.

With Miami's Office of Student Wellness and the Butler County General Health District, EMSA has placed {{ impact.fmt('narcan_kits') }} Narcan kits, {{ impact.fmt('test_strips') }} fentanyl test strips, {{ impact.fmt('condoms') }} condoms, and educational materials in {{ impact.fmt('frat_houses') }} fraternity houses.

Ohio law

  • Naloxone is available over the counter in Ohio.
  • Ohio's Good Samaritan Law gives limited immunity for minor possession offenses to the person who overdosed and the person who calls 911.

Ohio's Good Samaritan Law at the Ohio Department of Health
