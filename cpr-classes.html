CPR, First Aid, and Stop the Bleed classes

Free classes on campus, usually on Sundays. AHA courses are taught by EMSA's certified AHA instructors.

Courses

Stop the Bleed

Hands-on bleeding-control training

BLS (Basic Life Support)

The American Heart Association's BLS course.

Who it's for: Pre-health and healthcare students.

You receive: An AHA eCard, valid for two years.

Heartsaver CPR/First Aid

The American Heart Association's Heartsaver course in CPR and first aid.

Who it's for: Everyone.

You receive: An AHA eCard, valid for two years.

Stop the Bleed

Hands-on bleeding-control training. About 30 students have been trained so far.

Who it's for: Everyone.

Class details

Cost
Free.
Where
On campus.
When
Usually on Sundays. Dates are in the schedule below and on the Events calendar.
Your AHA eCard
BLS and Heartsaver participants receive an AHA eCard, purchased from and issued through the Therapeutic Professionals Training Center, typically within 20 business days. It is valid for two years, through the end of the month it was issued.
