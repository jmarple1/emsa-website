Hands-on training for a safer campus

EMS Alliance (EMSA) is a registered student organization at Miami University that teaches free CPR, First Aid, and Stop the Bleed classes and distributes naloxone and harm reduction supplies.

Join EMSA

  • Open to any Miami student
  • No requirements
  • No dues
  • About 1 hour a week

Our impact so far

  • 76+ students certified in CPR
  • 30 trained in Stop the Bleed
  • 200 Narcan kits and 2,000 fentanyl test strips across 25 fraternity houses

2024–25 New Student Organization of the Year · NCEMSF member and New Group Initiative grant recipient

What we do

  • Training

    Free AHA BLS and Heartsaver CPR/First Aid courses taught by EMSA's certified instructors, plus hands-on Stop the Bleed training.

    Explore training

  • Harm Reduction

    Free naloxone on campus through Ohio's Project DAWN program, and harm reduction supplies for fraternity houses.
